**Status: solved.** For every $N$, any $N$ lines in $\mathbb R^2$ satisfy the conjectured bound, with equality when the lines are split as evenly as possible between two perpendicular directions.

Set $A=\sum_{i<j}\arcsin|\langle x_i,x_j\rangle|$, so that $S=\binom N2\frac\pi2-A$ and the claim becomes $A\ge M(N,2)\frac\pi2$. The planar case is known [FVZ16, BM19]. The PDF gives a complete, self-contained proof.
