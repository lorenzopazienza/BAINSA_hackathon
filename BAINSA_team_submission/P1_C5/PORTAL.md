**Status: solved, for every $d$.** Any $d+2$ lines in $\mathbb R^d$ satisfy $S\le\big(\binom{d+2}2-2\big)\frac\pi2$, i.e. $A\ge\pi$. The proof is unconditional.

Two steps:
1. Saturation reduces an extremal configuration to orthogonal blocks: an isolated line, a coincident pair, three coincident lines, or a *cycle*.
2. A **new cycle lemma**, proved by a monotone potential along a spherical-trigonometric recursion.

Numerical checks (40k random cycles, $10^6$ points for the key inequality) are in the code folder. By-products: a two-line proof of C3 and a second proof of C4.
